# Auto Clicker - Mouse & Key Presser

Click or press keys automatically at any speed, at one spot or a sequence of them.

## Problem

People playing idle and sandbox games, or repeating a click in a work tool, want the mouse to click by itself. The Store leader is free and well liked but does one click target and no keys. The fastest-growing newcomer is a 170 MB download whose reviews report a forced review before use, an antivirus warning and a bandwidth-sharing prompt. The one polished app charges, by one-off or subscription, for several targets and key presses.

## Features (1.0)

- Click with the left, right or middle button, single or double, at an interval set in milliseconds or as clicks per second, sent with SendInput
- Click where the cursor is, or at a fixed point chosen with an on-screen picker
- Key presser: press or hold a chosen key on the same timer, alone or alongside the click
- Stop after a click count, after a time limit, or when the hotkey is pressed; optional start delay and random variation of interval and position
- Global start/stop hotkey that can be rebound, an always-on-top option, and a tray icon that shows when it is running
- Sequences: an ordered list of steps, each a point or key with its own delay, repeated in a loop
- Saved profiles with their own hotkeys
- Clear note that it cannot click inside windows running as administrator
- Free: everything above, with sequences of up to three steps and one saved profile. A one-off unlock (licence key, checked offline) removes both limits
- Licence module modelled on C:/devel/code/mp3-converter/src/license.rs (read that file): an Ed25519-signed key checked offline, with its own key prefix ACK1. Leave BUY_URL empty in 1.0, so the limits are off and no Buy or licence UI shows until a licence server exists; embed a well-formed placeholder public key marked to be replaced
- No ads, no review prompt, no network use beyond the update check; download under 15 MB

## Non-goals

- No macro recording of live mouse movement in 1.0
- No scripting, image recognition or pixel-colour triggers
- No hiding from or bypassing game anti-cheat, and no wording that suggests it
- No elevation, drivers or injected code
- No subscription
- No 'FPS unlocker' or other unrelated claims in the listing
