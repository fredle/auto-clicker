# Store listing - Auto Clicker & Key Presser

## Product name

Auto Clicker & Key Presser

## Short description

Click or press keys automatically at any speed, at one spot or a sequence of them.

## Description

Clicking the same spot a few thousand times is nobody's idea of fun. Idle and sandbox games, repetitive work tools and long form-filling jobs all ask for it, and the usual fixes are a tool that only does one click target, a huge download that wants a review or a bandwidth-sharing prompt before it starts, or a paid app that charges for the useful parts.

Auto Clicker & Key Presser is a small, quiet app that does the job. Pick a mouse button, choose how fast, and press F6. Click where the cursor is, or at a point you pick on screen. Add a key press that taps or holds on the same timer. Stop it after a number of clicks, after a time limit, or by pressing the hotkey again.

Need more than one spot? Build a sequence: an ordered list of clicks at points and key presses, each with its own wait, repeated in a loop. Save your favourite set-ups as profiles, each with a hotkey of its own.

The speed goes from one click an hour to a thousand a second, set in milliseconds or in clicks per second. Add a start delay, or a little random variation in timing and position when a perfectly regular rhythm is not what you want.

A tray icon next to the clock turns green while a run is active, and the hotkey works even when the window is minimised. The window can stay on top while you work.

No ads, no accounts, no review prompts and no telemetry. The only network use is the update check. It installs per user and does not ask for administrator rights.

Good to know: Windows does not allow normal apps to send clicks or keys into windows that run as administrator, so this app cannot click inside them.

## Product features

- Left, right or middle button, single or double click
- Speed from 1 ms to 1 hour between clicks, set in milliseconds or clicks per second
- Click at the cursor or at a fixed point chosen with an on-screen picker
- Tap or hold a key on the same timer, alone or alongside the click
- Stop after a click count, after a time limit, or with the hotkey
- Optional start delay before the first click
- Random variation of timing and position
- Sequences: ordered steps of clicks at points or key presses, each with its own wait, repeated in a loop
- Saved profiles, each with its own hotkey that loads and starts it
- Global start/stop hotkey (F6 by default) that you can rebind
- Hotkeys keep working while the window is minimised
- Tray icon that turns green while running
- Always-on-top window option
- Remembers your settings between sessions
- Small download, no ads, no accounts, no telemetry
- Installs per user, no administrator rights needed
- Updates itself in the background (can be switched off)

## Privacy

Stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AutoClicker: your settings (hotkey, click and key options, window preference, update preference) and any saved profiles. Nothing leaves your PC.

Network calls: only an update check against GitHub Releases, about once a day, which can be switched off with the "Automatically update" option. No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no advertising. The app does not read the screen, record mouse movement or log keystrokes; it only sends the clicks and keys you set up.
